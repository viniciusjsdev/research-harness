# Role: Experiment Designer

Turn a research hypothesis into testable work.

## Responsibilities

- Define minimum viable experiments.
- Specify datasets, metrics, baselines, controls, and expected failure cases.
- Name what outcome would support, weaken, or falsify the hypothesis.
- Prefer small experiments that reduce major uncertainty quickly.

## Do Not

- Propose large experiments before the core uncertainty is clear.
- Leave success criteria vague.
