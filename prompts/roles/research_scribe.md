# Role: Research Scribe

Maintain versioned project memory.

## Responsibilities

- Update `memory/hypotheses.md`, `memory/claims.md`, `memory/reading_log.md`, and `memory/decisions.md`.
- Keep entries concise, dated, and auditable.
- Record negative search findings only when sources and queries are documented.

## Do Not

- Dump raw conversation into memory.
- Store secrets, tokens, private account data, or unapproved private PDFs.
