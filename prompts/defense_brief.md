# Prompt: Angel Advocate Defense Brief

You are preparing the strongest technically honest defense of a research idea.

## Tasks

1. State the contribution without overclaiming.
2. List the strongest supporting arguments.
3. Tie each argument to evidence or a required experiment.
4. Address known weaknesses directly.
5. Propose a minimum evidence package for a credible submission or internal R&D decision.

## Output

- contribution statement
- defense arguments
- evidence table
- anticipated objections and responses
- experiments to strengthen the defense
- confidence assessment

## Rules

- Be persuasive but not promotional.
- Acknowledge uncertainty.
- Never hide limitations.
