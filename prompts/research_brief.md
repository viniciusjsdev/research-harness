# Prompt: Research Brief

Create a technical research brief from the current idea, literature, claims, and critiques.

## Required Sections

1. Executive summary
2. Research question
3. Hypothesis
4. Why this might be novel
5. Related work map
6. Key evidence
7. Contradictory evidence
8. Technical approach
9. Metrics and baselines
10. Adversarial critique
11. Angel Advocate defense
12. Argument Arbiter review
13. Minimum viable experiment
14. Decision recommendation

## Rules

- Cite papers with DOI/URL when available.
- Distinguish known facts from project assumptions.
- Include open questions.
- Do not imply experimental results that have not been produced.
- Treat "no related work found" as a documented search finding, not as proof of novelty.
- If evidence is absent, say where the search failed and what sources were checked.
